## Notes on the data
- **Two different demand measures.** Some sources give oversubscription (demand divided by shares offered, for example 37x). Others give the satisfaction rate (share of each order actually served, for example 23%). They are kept in separate columns and never mixed.
- **Conflicting dates.** CMGP Group's IPO is dated December 2024 by Dalil Finance and March 2024 by FNH. Disty Technologies is dated July 2022 by Boursenews and October 2022 by FNH. To confirm in each company's prospectus on ammc.ma.
- **Month-only dates** are stored as the first day of the month.
- **SGTM and T2S amounts** are the maximum amounts offered, not the final amounts.
- **Prices** are closing prices from the sources above. Returns are price returns only, without dividends.
- Most sources are financial press. The reference source for each IPO is its prospectus (note d'information) on ammc.ma.
